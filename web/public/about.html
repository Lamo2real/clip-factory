About Clip Factory

Clip Factory turns my gameplay recordings into short vertical clips and posts them to my own accounts when I press Keep.

Who runs it

Clip Factory is a private app run by one creator, for that creator only. There is no sign-up and there are no other users. It runs in the creator's own Amazon Web Services account.

How it works

  1. The creator pastes a link to a stream or video they have the right to clip, or uploads a recording.
  2. Clip Factory finds the best moments and makes short vertical clips with captions.
  3. The creator watches every clip and presses Keep or Throw away. Nothing is posted without Keep.
  4. Kept clips are posted to the creator's own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and X accounts through each platform's official API, after the creator connects each account with the platform's own login.
  5. Clip Factory reads the views and likes of those posts so the creator can see how each clip did.
