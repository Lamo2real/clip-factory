# Hooks and pacing for vertical shorts

- **Hook in two seconds.** Start in the middle of the action or with the problem already happening (the car already
  sliding, the coffee already falling). No logos, no slow build-up.
- **Something changes every 2–4 seconds.** A new action, a new face, a camera move or a cut. A still frame longer than
  that loses viewers.
- **Keep the subject clear of the platforms' buttons and captions.** In a vertical 9:16 video keep it in the middle
  third and above the bottom quarter; in a square 1:1 or wide 16:9 video keep a thin band clear at the edges and the
  bottom.
- **30–60 seconds is the sweet spot.** Shorter when the idea is one gag; longer only when every beat earns it.
- **Pay off the promise.** If the hook asks "what now?", the ending must answer it clearly.
- **A loopable ending** (the last frame leads back to the first) helps rewatches.
- **Captions that make people stay:** a question or a stake in the first line of the post caption, then the lesson or
  the joke; no clickbait the video does not deliver.
- **Every shape works.** These hooks are for the shape the owner picked: vertical 9:16, square 1:1 or wide 16:9.
  Plan each moment for the video's own frame (the brief says which).
