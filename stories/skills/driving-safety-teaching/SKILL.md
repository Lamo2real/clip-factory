# Teaching driving safety

- **Only checked advice.** Every instruction on screen comes from a shown step of the guidance topic you are given,
  in its order, and names its step id. If a step is hidden (sources disagree), do not show it; end with "Check your
  local road rules."
- **Show the right technique clearly.** For a skid seen from above, the car's rear and the steering must match the
  guidance exactly (for example: steer in the direction the rear is sliding). Get directions right before anything
  else.
- **Prevention first.** Most situations are best avoided: slow down, leave space, gentle inputs. Make that the
  takeaway when the guidance says so.
- **Calm, not scary.** Tension is fine; injuries, crashes shown as fun, or panic as the lesson are not.
- **Nothing to copy.** Never show stunts, drifting for fun, phone use while driving or breaking the law as cool.
- **Name the source** in the post description ("Based on UK Highway Code rule 119").
