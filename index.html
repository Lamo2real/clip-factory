Lamo Clip Factory

Lamo Clip Factory is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It helps one creator cut short vertical clips from videos and streams the creator has the right to clip, and publish them to the creator's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts.

How it works

  1. The owner signs in to their own accounts using each platform's official login (for example Login Kit on TikTok).
  2. The tool prepares clips, with captions and a text hook.
  3. The owner reviews every clip and approves it. Nothing is published without that approval.
  4. Approved clips are uploaded to the owner's own accounts with each platform's official posting API.

This is a private tool for a single creator. It is not offered to the public, there are no other users, and nothing is installed on anyone else's device.
