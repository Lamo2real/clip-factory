## Out of scope for this phase

- Posting automatically the clips that were kept before a platform was connected (they stay "Waiting for connection";
  [roadmap](ROADMAP.md#phase-2-social-integrations)).
- Webhook signature checks and acting on webhook events.
- OpenSearch Serverless (an option, not deployed).
- Joining or applying to Content Rewards campaigns inside Clip Factory (that stays the owner's own tap on Content
  Rewards; its tools offer no way to do it, and a robot on its site would break Whop's terms). Reading and following
  joined campaigns is in scope (BR-25), and so is finding campaigns that match what he wants from Content Rewards'
  public pages (I-50).
- Content Rewards campaigns that want drafts first or the brand's own sound, and sending proof of views for the owner
  (Clip Factory reminds him and says how).
- Money from TikTok, Instagram and X (their tools tell apps nothing about it); they say "not available" with where to
  look (BR-27). What left Content Rewards for Whop and the bank shows only through Whop's own payouts, once "Connect
  Whop" is on (BR-30 AC5).
- Reading his bank (what reached it): it waits for the owner's choice of source (BR-30 AC6, I-60). Typing in amounts
  for places with no API stays refused (I-59).
- Turning a normal clip into a campaign clip in place (a copy is made instead, BR-28 AC5), and campaigns that want
  their own picture on top of the video (a hard stop "Pick another campaign").
- Auto-posting to TikTok (TikTok's rules want the creator's own pick for each post; he posts it himself with **Post
  it yourself**), and auto-posting campaign clips while Content Rewards can't take clips from Clip Factory by itself
  (they wait for his Upload, BR-29 AC4 and AC5).
- More than one user.
- A custom domain.
- Signed and notarized desktop builds, and a store listing for phones (NFR-11 AC5, [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md#later)).
