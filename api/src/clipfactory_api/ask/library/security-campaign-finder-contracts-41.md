## Campaign finder (CONTRACTS §41)

The finder reads Content Rewards' **public** campaign pages (what anyone sees logged out) a few times a day and
matches them to what the owner wants ([CONTRACTS.md §41](CONTRACTS.md#41-campaign-finder-campaigns-that-match-what-you-want-owner-request-2026-11-21),
[ADR-65](DECISIONS.md#adr-65-campaign-finder-content-rewards-public-pages-read-politely-joining-stays-the-owners-own-tap),
[ARCHITECTURE.md flow 21](ARCHITECTURE.md#21-campaign-finder-contracts-41-adr-65)). Joining stays his own tap on
Content Rewards; nothing drives Content Rewards' or Whop's site for him. It ships switched off (`CR_FINDER=off`,
every finder route 503) until all its parts have merged.

| What | How it is protected |
|---|---|
| Outside pages, read by a Lambda with no secret | Only `cf-prod-campaigns` reads them: its role has **no `secretsmanager:*` action at all** (a Terraform test), no MCP client and no Content Rewards token, so a hostile page can't reach any key. It makes no model call for the finder. The api's finder routes read only stored items; the one MCP call (Ask Content Rewards) and the one public JSON read on "I joined: add it" are the owner's own actions in `cf-prod-api`, as in §38. |
| robots.txt honoured | `robots.txt` is read at most once a day (64 KB, 8 s) and every request's path must be allowed by the stored copy (RFC 9309: the `clipfactory` group, else `*`; longest match wins). Its 21 Nov 2026 file disallows `/api/` and allows `/sitemap.xml` and `/c/campaigns/{uuid}`, so only those are read. If robots.txt stops allowing them the finder reads nothing (`not_allowed`); with no readable copy at most 7 days old it reads nothing (`down`). No `/api/…` path is ever read on a schedule, not even on "Look again now" (§38.10's rule, unchanged). |
| Every read anonymous, only from AWS | The reads come only from `cf-prod-campaigns` in AWS, never from the owner's Mac, the desktop app or his browser, so none can be linked to his Content Rewards or Whop account (Whop's guidelines can hold back the payouts of accounts that use bots or crawlers). Each request is a `GET` with `User-Agent: ClipFactory/1` and **no cookie, no `Authorization`, no `Referer`**, no account or organization id. One look reads once for every owner (one shared cache under pk `CRPUBLIC`). |
| Nothing about the owner sent | No owner id, email, want, match or campaign of his is ever in a URL, header or body sent to Content Rewards' pages; the requests are the same for every owner. Ask Content Rewards sends one fixed literal (`finderapi.ASK_MESSAGE`, no personal data, URL or token) through his own signed-in MCP `send_feedback`, only on his tap, at most once per 30 days. |
| Polite and capped | One request at a time, at least 1 s apart; at most 40 campaign pages and 100 s a look; at most 6 reading looks a day, 30 minutes apart, counted by the lease before the first request (so a crash can't add looks); "Look again now" at most once per 30 minutes and 4 times a day per owner. 429 and 503 back off (at least 1 hour or `Retry-After`, doubled, at most 24 hours), 403 or a bot wall waits 24 hours and becomes `blocked` after 3 looks in a row. Body caps: robots.txt 64 KB, sitemaps 4 MB, pages 2 MB. |
| The SSRF guard | Every request goes through §38.10's `fetch.get` (https only, public addresses only) with host exactly `contentrewards.com` and port 443; at most 3 redirects, each hop checked the same way (a hop elsewhere ends that read). The sitemap keeps only exact `https://contentrewards.com/c/campaigns/{uuid}` entries (no query, no fragment). Tests make any real DNS look-up or socket fail in the `test_finder_*` modules. |
| Never read | `/api/…` paths, the Discover page, `?group=1` pages, invite links, anything on Whop, any signed-in page, images, banners, brand logos and reference files (a match shows a round letter; the brand picture is copied only after he adds the campaign, §38.10). The parts of a page about other creators (`topClips`, `topEarners`, `chartPoints`, every username) are dropped while parsing and never stored or logged. |
| Brand text plain | Campaign names, brands and category names from the pages are untrusted brand text: shown as plain escaped text, at most 300 characters, never HTML and never a link Clip Factory didn't make (the only links are its own `https://contentrewards.com/c/campaigns/{uuid}` and Discover, opened with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`). Descriptions and briefs are read only for the overlay, sound, viewer-country and language signals, in pure code, and are **not stored**; the words a match shows are Clip Factory's own sentences from fixed templates. |
| The shared cache | `CRPUBLIC` items (`CAMP#`, `PUB#`, `STATE`) hold only public campaign data and the reader's state, no owner data; they never travel on the WebSocket (the broadcast takes only `USER#` keys) and expire 60 days after their read. The owner's `CRFIND` item travels as one `contentrewards` event with `id: "finder"`. |
| Logs | `finder.read` lines carry the status class only, never a body; a format change is `finder.format_changed`. |
| Stopping it | If Content Rewards or Whop asks Clip Factory to stop reading, `cr_finder_enabled` goes back to `false` the same day in a one-line pull request ([DEPLOYMENT.md](DEPLOYMENT.md#campaign-finder-contracts-41)); the stored items stay and nothing on Content Rewards changes. |
