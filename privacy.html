Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Lamo Clip Factory ("the app") is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner. It has no website accounts, no servers that store user data, and no other users.

What the app receives

When the owner connects an account (TikTok, YouTube or Instagram) through that platform's official login, the platform gives the app:

  • an access token that lets the app post videos to that account on the owner's behalf, and
  • basic profile information of the connected account: its username, display name and account id.

How it is used

This information is used only to show which accounts are connected and to upload videos that the owner has reviewed and approved. The app does not read the owner's private messages, followers or other accounts, and does not use any data for advertising or profiling.

Where it is stored

Access tokens and keys are stored encrypted on the owner's computer, with the encryption key kept in the operating system's keychain. They are not sent to any server operated by the developer.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared or disclosed to third parties. The app only communicates with the platforms' own official APIs (TikTok, Google/YouTube, Meta/Instagram) in order to log in and upload videos.

Cookies and analytics

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. The app has no analytics.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in the connected platform's settings (TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions; Instagram and Facebook: Settings, Business integrations). The owner can also disconnect an account inside the app, which deletes its stored token.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is not offered to the public.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above is updated. Questions can be sent through the GitHub issues page.
